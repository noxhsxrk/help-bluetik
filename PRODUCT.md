# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Thai X.com content creators with X Verify (blue tick) who want to collaborate on engagement to boost impressions and monetization. They need a quick, distraction-free way to share their posts and interact with others' posts to build mutually beneficial engagement loops.

## Product Purpose
"ติ๊กฟ้าช่วยติ๊กฟ้า" is a high-fidelity, minimalist engagement-exchange platform that helps verified X.com creators coordinate their sharing, liking, reposting, quoting, and mentioning. By automating validation via X SSO/API and providing hourly rate limits and a leaderboard, the system builds an authentic community-led engagement group that is secure, fair, and effective.

## Brand Personality
- Minimalist Editorial
- Stark, crisp, clean, distraction-free
- 3-word personality: Stark, Focused, High-fidelity
- Emotional goals: Focus, trust, efficiency, professional collaboration

## Anti-references
- NOT a cheap, spammy 'get-rich-quick' bot farm style with flashy banners and low contrast.
- NOT a cluttered or confusing layout that makes navigation difficult for non-technical users.
- Avoid generic web-app dashboards filled with empty colored stats, glassmorphism, or colorful gradient text.

## Design Principles
- Stark Contrast and Restraint: Rely on absolute black, white, and a single accent or crisp borders instead of colorful blobs.
- Extreme Clarity and Utility: Focus on post interactions and stats. Text wraps beautifully; layout respects the grid.
- Guarded Community: First-class registration approval flows that feel exclusive and secure.
- Seamless Actionability: Clear direct actions for reposting/liking that respect user intent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA contrast ratio compliance (>= 4.5:1 for body and placeholder texts).
- Responsive layout with high readability on both mobile and desktop viewports.
- Keyboard navigable interaction cards.
